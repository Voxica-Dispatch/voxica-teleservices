Privacy information

How Voxica handles information submitted through this website.

Privacy notice under review. This page describes the current website form setup. It should be reviewed for legal completeness before a broader launch.

Information you provide

When you submit a business inquiry, talent-network interest, or separate Arise-interest form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, state, business details, experience, preferences, and optional message. Please do not submit Social Security numbers, government identification, financial account details, medical information, or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Voxica uses submitted information to review and respond to inquiries, assess potential service fit, and follow up about the opportunity pathway you selected. Submitting interest does not guarantee a job, client agreement, or placement.

Form service and email

Website forms currently use FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, to send submissions to Voxica at voxicateleservices@gmail.com. Your information is transmitted through that service and may be processed according to its practices. Do not submit information if you do not wish to use this form delivery method.

Retention and requests

Voxica is establishing its data retention and access procedures. To ask about information you submitted or request deletion, contact voxicateleservices@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable requirements.

Changes

We may update this notice as the website and our operations develop.
