Privacy information

How Voxica handles information submitted through this website.

Privacy notice — operational details under review. This notice explains the website's current collection methods. Data retention periods, any analytics or cookies, and other legal requirements should be confirmed before broader promotion.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

Voxica Teleservices is a customer support business operated by Voxica LLC. Questions about this notice or your submission can be sent to voxicateleservices@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on the form you choose, we may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, state, business name, requested support channels, estimated business support needs, customer support experience, availability, equipment information, written messages, and an optional résumé. Please do not send Social Security numbers, identification documents, banking details, medical information, or other sensitive personal information through these forms.

Why we use information

We use submissions to respond to business inquiries, assess potential service fit, review representative interest, and communicate about the separate Arise-interest pathway. Submitting a form does not guarantee employment, placement, or a business agreement.

Third-party form providers

Business inquiries and Arise-interest responses are collected through Formspree. Representative applications and optional résumé uploads are collected through Jotform. These providers process information under their own policies. Form notifications are sent to Voxica's business Gmail inbox; information may therefore also be processed by the relevant email provider. Review each provider's privacy information before submitting.

Website technology

Like many websites, the hosting provider and embedded services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and access logs to operate and protect their services. The embedded Jotform application may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Voxica has not yet completed a separate cookie and analytics inventory; this notice will be updated if additional tracking tools are introduced.

Access, retention, and security

Authorized Voxica personnel may review submissions and résumé files for the purposes described above. Information is stored using the form providers and associated email systems. Voxica is still defining a formal retention schedule and deletion workflow. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your questions and requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, email voxicateleservices@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your submission, but do not email sensitive identity documents. Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law and any legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Children

This website is intended for business contacts and adults exploring work opportunities. Please do not submit information about children through these forms.

Updates

We may revise this notice as our practices change. The date above will be updated when material revisions are published.
